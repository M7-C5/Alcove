### Features

- 4-stage progressive educational interior design curriculum.

- Interactive Mascot AI companion with fast-path micro-reactions and stage evaluation gates.
- Dual layout architecture: `baselineLayout` (immutable scan) and `workingLayout` (mutable draft).
- Automated build, test, and thermal cleanup verification tooling (`scripts/verify.sh`).
